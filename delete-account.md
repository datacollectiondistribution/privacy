# Delete your 40NSEW account

This page explains how to delete your **40NSEW** account (developer: **Shez Care Foundation**) and the personal data linked to it.

---

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **info@shezcarefdn.org**
2. Use the subject line: **Delete my account**
3. Send it **from the email address you use to sign in to 40NSEW**, so we can confirm the account is yours.
4. Optionally, include your full name and mobile number registered in the app.

We will reply to confirm your request and complete the deletion **within 30 days**. You will receive an email once your account has been deleted.

You do not need to have the app installed to request deletion.

---

## Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete:

- Your account details: name, email address, mobile number and password
- Your profile photo and profile address
- Location saved with your profile
- Your sign-in and session data

---

## Data that may be kept

Some information may be kept after your account is deleted:

- **Donation and distribution records** you created (for example, food or clothing given to a donee) may be kept in **anonymized form**, with your name and contact details removed, so the charity program can continue to report on aid delivered.
- **Donee records** you registered belong to the aid program and the people it supports, so they are not deleted with your account. Your personal details are removed from them.
- Information we are **required to keep by law** (for example, for accounting or legal reasons) is kept only for as long as the law requires and is then deleted.
- **Crash and analytics data** collected by Google Firebase is not linked to your name or email and is deleted automatically by Firebase after its standard retention period (up to 14 months).
