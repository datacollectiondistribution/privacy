# Privacy Policy — 40NSEW

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026
**Last updated:** 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Shez Care Foundation** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the **40NSEW** mobile application (the "App"), a platform for registering donees and recording the collection and distribution of donations such as food, clothing, education, health and shelter support.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide
- **Account information:** first name, last name, email address, mobile number, password, and profile photo.
- **Donee and family information** (entered by you or by authorized field staff): name, date of birth, gender, address, mobile number, family/dependant details, income information, and whether medical attention is needed.
- **Photos:** pictures taken with the camera or selected from your device, such as profile photos, photos used to identify a donee or dependant, and photos recorded as proof of a donation or distribution.
- **Donation and distribution records:** details of items donated, received or distributed, with the date and place.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Location:** precise (GPS) and approximate location, used to tag donation and distribution records with their place, to show nearby locations on maps, and to let you pick an address. If you grant background location permission, location may also be collected while the App is not open, only to complete location-tagged records.
- **Device and usage information:** device model, operating system version, app version, network state, and how you interact with the App.
- **Crash and diagnostic data:** crash logs and performance information, used to find and fix problems.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- create and manage your account and verify your identity (including PIN and email verification);
- register donees and their dependants, and record donations and distributions;
- confirm that aid reaches the intended recipients, using photos and location;
- show maps, addresses and nearby locations;
- send service messages such as verification codes and account notices;
- keep the App secure and prevent fraud or misuse;
- analyze usage and fix crashes to improve the App;
- meet legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Location (fine, coarse, background) | Tag records with location, show maps, pick addresses |
| Camera | Take profile, donee and distribution photos |
| Photos / storage | Select and save images used in the App |
| Accounts / credentials | Sign in with your Google account |
| Internet and network state | Communicate with our servers and detect when you are offline |
| Run at startup | Restore scheduled background tasks after the device restarts |

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. Some features may stop working without it.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

We share information only in these cases:
- **Organizations you work with:** donation and distribution records are shared with the charitable organization that runs the program you take part in.
- **Service providers** that process data for us under contract:
  - **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics) — sign-in, usage analytics, crash reporting;
  - **Google Maps Platform and Places** — maps, location search and addresses;
  - **Cloud hosting providers** (for example Microsoft Azure) — storage of App data on our servers.
- **Legal reasons:** when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the people we serve, or the public.
- **Business transfers:** if we are part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

---

## 5. Data Security

Data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and sensitive values are encrypted further inside the App. Access to personal data is limited to authorized personnel. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active, or as long as it is needed to provide the service, keep donation and distribution records, and meet legal, accounting or reporting obligations. After that, we delete or anonymize it.

---

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate information (you can edit most of your profile in the App);
- ask us to delete your account and personal data;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off location or camera permissions.

### Account and data deletion
To delete your account and the personal data linked to it, email **info@shezcarefdn.org** with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address registered in the App. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days. Some records may be kept where the law requires it, or in anonymized form for aggregate reporting.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and children may not create accounts. Information about minors (for example a donee's dependants) is entered only by authorized staff or a parent or guardian, and only to deliver aid. If you believe a child has given us personal information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including where our hosting and service providers operate. When we transfer data, we take reasonable steps to protect it as described in this policy.

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
